# Quantum Computing Blog

**What is this?** 

A detailed tutorial on quantum computing and quantum information theory by an at the moment UvA/VU CompSci master's student in Amsterdam, in a blog format. If you just need a short summary of everything - check out [the cheatsheet](/qc-csheet) with the main formulas and concepts from this blog. If you want this blog in a video format - check out the YouTube playlist (soon). If you are not specialized in this field and just want to understand the main concepts from a general audience perspective - check out [The Science Guild](https://www.thescienceguild.org).

**Aim**

To actually learn and understand qauantum computing and quantum information theory, analyze and compare sources, keep a more scientific but also practical format (like the [PennyLane demos](https://pennylane.ai/demonstrations)), and, of course, pass the Quantum Computing, Quantum Information Theory and Quantum Hardware courses at UvA.

**How does it work?**

1. I read one lecture lecture notes from both QC and QIT courses at UvA.
2. I solve the homework problems, analyze and research all the uncertainties, compare sources, their notation etc.
3. I make a tutorial-blog post with all the conclusions, update the cheatsheet.
4. I make a YouTube video about it.
5. At the end of each month I **consider** making a mini lecture at The Science Guild, summarizing interesting and important things.
6. Repeat this until the QC and QIT courses end, then switch to QH course, then switch to lab work or internship work, etc.
